Our story

Learning without barriers

Lumina E-Library replaces slow, paper-based record keeping with a simple online system. Librarians add and organise titles; students and teachers search, read, watch and download - without queues or lost records.

  • One-time registration for every member
  • Search by title, category, author or publisher
  • Read books online in the browser
  • Video tutorials grouped by category
  • Downloads where the librarian allows them

Quick answers

FAQ

Do I need an account to browse?

No. Guests can view titles and library information. Reading and downloads need a free account.

Can I download every book?

Only books or categories the administrator has marked as downloadable show a download button.

I forgot my password.

Use the Forgot password page to request a reset link.
